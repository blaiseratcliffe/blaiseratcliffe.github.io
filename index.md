## Projects

### Research tools

**MAGPlot** · [Open Canada](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/8824392d-464e-413d-8bde-eaed61c79743)
Pan-Canadian repository of harmonized forest ground-plot data: approximately 14 million tree measurements from over 52,000 plots, spanning the 1920s to the 2020s. I develop the interactive Shiny explorer for filtering and visualizing plots across provinces and ecozones. I also build MAGPlotQC, an R package for data validation and quality control.

**breedR** · [GitHub](https://github.com/blaiseratcliffe/breedr)
Modernized fork of an R package for quantitative genetics, rebuilt around the current BLUPF90+ program suite. The original relied on deprecated binaries and had no genomic tools. This version adds single-step genomic selection (ssGBLUP), genome-wide association studies, Bayesian variance estimation, and wrappers for the rest of the BLUPF90 toolkit, including data preparation, parentage verification and prediction validation.

### Side projects

**FerryHawk** · [ferryhawk.ca](https://ferryhawk.ca)
Free alerts when sold-out BC Ferries sailings open up. Monitors major routes and sends instant notifications when spots appear.

**CrateMine** · [cratemine.app](https://cratemine.app)
Finds trending tracks and rising artists in a city's underground music scene, using SoundCloud's social graph to map scene networks. Two discovery modes, keyword search and scene discovery, feed playlists you can merge and curate. Built with Next.js, React, and TypeScript.
